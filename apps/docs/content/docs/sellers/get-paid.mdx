title: Get paid
description: Set a price on your offer, fund the fee payer with devnet SOL, and see where your devnet USDC arrives.
---

In the beta, a seller can charge for its model on Solana **devnet**, in Circle devnet USDC. Devnet USDC has no
market value: the beta tests the payment path, not real income. Mainnet is not open.

## What you can sell [#beta-scope]

| How you sell                     | Who can buy                | Tools (agents) | Availability                           |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Free**                         | Any buyer                  | Yes            | Every seller                           |
| **Priced, paid per request**     | Any buyer                  | No, text only  | Every seller; this page                |
| Priced, reusable channel (pilot) | One buyer paired by Gyblos | Yes            | Pilot only; Gyblos sets it up with you |

Coding agents such as Codex or Claude Code use tools, so they buy from free offers or from the pilot. A priced
per-request offer serves plain text conversations. To tune a free offer for agents, see
[answer and context length](/en/sellers/models-offers#answer-and-context-length).

## Prerequisites [#requirements]

- A registered seller ([Register and publish](/en/sellers/register-publish)).
- The installer's **Qwen3 0.6B** offer. A buyer must be able to recount a priced model's tokens with its
  public tokenizer, and only `qwen3:0.6b` qualifies. Any other priced model stops startup with
  `SELLER_PAID_METERING_UNRECOUNTED`.
- The seller's payment keys, which the installer creates: the **owner** receives your USDC and the
  **fee payer** pays the transaction costs. What each key does, and how to back them up, is in
  [Identity and backups](/en/sellers/identity#keys).

You need no USDC to sell. Buyers pay you in devnet USDC.

## 1. Set a price [#set-a-price]

In the offer in `config.toml`, replace the `free = {...}` line with a `price` in USDC per million tokens:

```toml
[[offers]]
model = "qwen3:0.6b"
reasoning = ["off"]
engine = { kind = "ollama", url = "http://127.0.0.1:11434/" }
price = { input_per_mtok = "0.05", output_per_mtok = "0.20", currency = "USDC" }
```

Prices have at most 6 decimals, and input and output cannot both be zero. An offer is either free or priced,
never both. You set the net price; buyers see it **plus the 4% Gyblos fee**.

## 2. Fund the fee payer [#fund-the-fee-payer]

Run `gyblos doctor` (Docker seller: `docker compose run --rm seller gyblos doctor`) and copy the `fee payer`
address. Send it about 0.05 devnet SOL from [the devnet faucet](https://faucet.solana.com).

The fee payer spends devnet SOL on:

- about 0.002 SOL once for each missing token account (your USDC payout account and the fee accounts), which
  the seller creates at startup;
- about 0.00002 SOL in transaction fees per paid request with the default settings (an estimate, not a
  maximum);
- about 0.002 SOL of rent per paid request, held until a cleanup recovers it. After a restart, some older rent
  may not come back automatically.

  0.05 SOL is a starting balance, not a budget: many requests in a row use it up fast, so fund more if you
  expect heavy use. If it runs low, the seller does not start (`SELLER_PAYMENT_ACCOUNTS_UNAVAILABLE`) or cannot
  collect. Check its balance regularly and top it up from the faucet.

## 3. Apply and verify

Apply the change as described in
[Operate and update](/en/sellers/operate#apply-configuration-or-environment-changes). Before restarting,
`gyblos config validate` shows the price on the offer line, for example `0.05 in / 0.20 out USDC per Mtok`. After the restart,
`gyblos doctor` lists it under `Listing:` as `offer[1] paid qwen3:0.6b`, and `gyblos seller status` reports
`RUNNING`.

## How a buyer pays you [#how-payment-works]

Each paid request is paid on its own:

1. The buyer signs a maximum for the request and deposits it in a payment channel.
2. Your seller answers and measures the usage. The buyer verifies the answer it received.
3. Your seller settles the channel: the owner receives your price, Gyblos receives its fee and the rest
   returns to the buyer.

After each answer, the seller tries to settle it; confirmation can take time or stay pending. After a
restart, the seller tries to recover pending charges whose amounts it saved, without repeating the answer or
charging twice. Keep its data and keys, and keep the fee payer funded: network problems, missing SOL or an
expired channel can prevent collection.

## See your earnings [#see-earnings]

Your earnings are the devnet USDC balance of the **owner** address. Search for that address on
[Solana Explorer](https://explorer.solana.com/?cluster=devnet) with the devnet cluster selected. A payment
can take a few minutes to appear.

`gyblos seller status` also shows the paid requests of the last 24 hours: how many settled, their gross USDC
(including the Gyblos fee) and how many are still settling. These recent totals are not your lifetime
earnings or your wallet balance. An unsafe or invalid payment ledger shows as unavailable.

## Reusable channel pilot [#reusable-channel-pilot]

A reusable channel lets one deposit pay for many answers, with tools. It settles when the buyer asks, the
deposit runs out, the terms expire or the channel is idle. In the beta it runs only as a pilot with a buyer
paired by Gyblos, and not on Windows. To join, ask in the [Gyblos Telegram](https://t.me/gyblos); Gyblos
configures it with you.

`max_sessions` in `config.toml` sets how many reusable sessions your seller keeps open at once, from 1 to 16
(default 16). It counts open payment sessions, not connections:

```toml
[limits]
max_sessions = 16
```

## Back to free [#back-to-free]

An all-free seller does not run payment recovery or cleanup. Before you switch your last priced offer back to
free, let pending payments settle and close any pilot channels. Keep your payment data and keys, and contact
Gyblos if anything stays unresolved.

Then replace `price = {...}` with `free = {...}` and apply the change.
