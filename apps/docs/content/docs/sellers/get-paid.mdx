title: Get paid
description: Charge for an offer on devnet, fund the fee payer, and know when and where your USDC arrives.

During the beta a seller can charge for its model on **devnet**, in Circle devnet USDC. Devnet USDC has no
market value: the beta tests the payment path, not real income. Mainnet is not open.

## What you can sell during the beta [#beta-scope]

| How you sell                     | Who can buy                | Tools (agents) | Status                                        |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Free**                         | Any buyer                  | Yes            | Open to every seller                          |
| **Priced, paid per request**     | Any buyer                  | No, text only  | Open to every seller; this page               |
| Priced, reusable channel (pilot) | One buyer paired by Gyblos | Yes            | Paired pilot only; Gyblos sets it up with you |

Coding agents such as Codex or Claude Code use tools, so during the beta they buy from **free** offers or
from the paired pilot. A priced offer serves plain text conversations.

Agents send long prompts, thousands of tokens. To serve them for free, raise the engine's `context_tokens`
(up to 32768, see [configuration](/en/reference/configuration)) and use a fast machine, with Apple Silicon or
a GPU. A CPU-only server can be too slow, as it was in our rehearsal.

## What you need [#requirements]

- A registered seller ([register and publish](/en/sellers/register-publish)).
- The pinned **Qwen3 0.6B** offer that the installer sets up. A priced offer needs a model whose tokens the
  buyer can recount with its public tokenizer; today that is only `qwen3:0.6b`. Any other priced model
  stops startup with `SELLER_PAID_METERING_UNRECOUNTED`.
- About **0.05 devnet SOL** on the **fee payer** (see [below](#fund-the-fee-payer)). The owner's SOL pays
  for registration only.

You do not need USDC to sell. Buyers pay you in devnet USDC.

## The four keys [#keys]

The installer creates four Solana keys. They live in the seller's role folder: the Docker volume at
`/data/seller/` with Docker, `~/.gyblos/seller/` without Docker, `%USERPROFILE%\.gyblos\seller\` on Windows.

| Key        | File               | What it does                                                                    |
| ---------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Node key   | `node.key`         | The seller's network identity (NodeId). Buyers connect to it.                   |
| Owner      | `owner.json`       | Registered in 8004, pays registration, and **receives your USDC** (the payout). |
| Authorizer | `authorizer.json`  | Signs payment permissions for the seller. Holds no funds.                       |
| Fee payer  | `facilitator.json` | Pays the Solana fees and rent of your payment transactions. Holds devnet SOL.   |

`gyblos doctor` prints the public addresses of the owner and the fee payer under the seller identity. They
are safe to share. The files are private keys: never share or upload them. How to keep a copy is in
[identity](/en/sellers/identity#back-up-keys).

## Set a price [#set-a-price]

Replace `free = {...}` on the offer in `config.toml` with a price, in USDC per million tokens:

```toml
[[offers]]
model = "qwen3:0.6b"
reasoning = ["off"]
engine = { kind = "ollama", url = "http://127.0.0.1:11434/" }
price = { input_per_mtok = "0.05", output_per_mtok = "0.20", currency = "USDC" }
```

Prices have at most 6 decimals and both sides cannot be zero. An offer is either free or priced, not both.
Then apply the change:

- Docker: `docker compose up -d --force-recreate seller` in `~/gyblos-seller`.
- Without Docker (macOS, Linux, Windows): run the installer again (`sh install.sh`, or `install.ps1`). It
  keeps your keys and restarts the seller with the new file.

Buyers see your price **plus the 4% Gyblos fee**. You set the net price; the fee is added on top.

## Fund the fee payer [#fund-the-fee-payer]

Run `gyblos doctor` (Docker: `docker compose run --rm seller gyblos doctor`) and copy the `fee payer`
address. Send it about 0.05 SOL from [the devnet faucet](https://faucet.solana.com).

The fee payer pays:

- once, about 0.002 SOL for each missing token account (your USDC payout account and the fee accounts),
  which the seller creates at startup;
- the transaction fees of every paid request: about 0.00002 SOL per request with the default settings
  (an estimate, not a guaranteed maximum);
- the temporary rent of each request's channel, recovered later by a cleanup. A restart can lose track of
  older rent, so not all of it is guaranteed to come back automatically.

In our devnet rehearsal, each paid request held about **0.002 SOL** as rent until cleanup. With many
requests in a row, 0.05 SOL goes down fast; if you expect heavy use, fund more.

0.05 SOL is a starting balance, not a fixed budget. If it runs low, the seller does not start
(`SELLER_PAYMENT_ACCOUNTS_UNAVAILABLE`) or cannot collect. Check its balance regularly and top it up from the
faucet.

## How a buyer pays you [#how-payment-works]

Each paid request is paid on its own:

1. The buyer signs a maximum for the request and deposits it in a payment channel.
2. Your seller answers and measures the usage to request payment. The buyer verifies the answer it
   received; per-request payment does not wait for a new buyer signature approving the final usage.
3. Your seller settles that channel on chain: your share goes to the owner as USDC, the 4% fee to Gyblos,
   and the rest of the deposit back to the buyer.

After each inference, the seller attempts to settle the delivered usage. Confirmation may take time or
remain pending. After a restart, it attempts to recover pending charges whose amounts were saved, without
repeating inference or charging twice. Keep its data and keys and fund the fee payer; network problems,
insufficient SOL or expiry can prevent collection.

## See your earnings [#see-earnings]

`gyblos seller status` shows activity, not money. Your earnings are the devnet USDC balance of the
**owner** address. Look it up on [Solana Explorer](https://explorer.solana.com/?cluster=devnet) (search
the owner address, devnet cluster). A payment can take a few minutes to appear.

## Reusable channel pilot [#reusable-channel-pilot]

A reusable channel lets one deposit pay for many answers, with tools, and settles when the buyer asks, the
deposit runs out, the terms expire or the channel is idle. During the beta it runs only as a pilot with a
buyer paired by Gyblos, and not on Windows. If you want to join the pilot, ask in your invitation channel;
Gyblos configures it with you. How many reusable sessions your seller keeps open at once is set in
`config.toml`:

```toml
[limits]
max_sessions = 16
```

The default is 16, from 1 to 16. It counts open payment sessions, not connections.

## Back to free [#back-to-free]

Replace `price = {...}` with `free = {...}` again and apply the change as above.
